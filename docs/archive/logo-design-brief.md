# neo4all — Logo Design Brief

**Project**: neo4all — AI-Powered Graph Extraction & Curation Platform
**Date**: 2026-02-28
**Deliverable**: 3 logo proposals + favicon + monochrome variants

---

## 1. What neo4all Is

neo4all is a developer-facing web application that transforms unstructured documents into curated knowledge graphs stored in Neo4j. Users upload documents; AI agents extract entities and relationships; a governed pipeline (Proposal → Human Approval → Deterministic Diff → Execution → Audit) ensures every graph mutation is traceable and human-approved. The product sits at the intersection of **graph databases**, **AI/LLM processing**, and **data governance**.

### Key Concepts to Express

- **Graph structure** — nodes, edges, connections, networks
- **AI assistance** — intelligence augmenting human judgment, not replacing it
- **Universality** — "for all" (the name literally means "Neo4j for all")
- **Curation & trust** — governed, auditable, human-in-the-loop
- **Transformation** — raw documents become structured knowledge

### Personality

| Trait                      | Expression                            |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Technical but approachable | Not enterprise-cold; not startup-cute |
| Precise                    | Clean geometry, deliberate spacing    |
| Trustworthy                | Solid, balanced, no gimmicks          |
| Modern                     | Current design language, not retro    |

## 2. Three Proposal Directions

### Proposal A — "Connected Nodes"

**Concept**: A minimal graph motif — 3–4 circles (nodes) connected by clean lines (edges) — that forms a recognizable mark. One node is visually distinct (larger, filled, or differently colored) representing the "curated" central insight. The wordmark sits alongside.

**Mood**: Technical, precise, diagrammatic. Feels like it belongs in a developer tool or data platform. Think: the simplicity of the Git logo meets the structure of a network diagram.

**Why it works**: Directly represents the core domain (graph databases). Immediately legible to the target audience. Scales cleanly to favicon (the central node alone or the simplest 2–3 node cluster).

---

### Proposal B — "Document-to-Graph Transformation"

**Concept**: A single continuous mark that transitions from a document shape (left/top) into a graph shape (right/bottom). This could be a page silhouette whose corner or edge dissolves into connected nodes, or an abstract shape where straight/rectangular lines morph into circular/networked forms.

**Mood**: Dynamic, narrative, process-oriented. Communicates the core value proposition (documents become knowledge graphs) in one glyph.

**Why it works**: Tells the product story without words. The transformation motif is distinctive and memorable. Favicon crops to the graph-side of the mark.

---

### Proposal C — "The Lens / Eye"

**Concept**: An abstract lens, prism, or eye shape suggesting that neo4all *sees into* documents and reveals hidden structure. The interior of the lens contains a subtle graph motif (nodes/edges). The overall silhouette is simple and geometric — a circle, hexagon, or rounded triangle.

**Mood**: Intelligent, perceptive, refined. More abstract and brandable than the literal graph motif. Suggests AI "vision" and analytical depth.

**Why it works**: More distinctive and ownable than a generic graph icon. The container shape (lens/eye) gives strong favicon presence. Interior graph detail rewards closer inspection at larger sizes.

## 4. Typography

### Wordmark

- The word "neo4all" is always lowercase
- The "4" is a digit, not spelled out — it is part of the brand name
- A geometric or grotesque sans-serif is preferred (e.g., Inter, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, Geist, or a custom letterset)
- The typeface must have clear distinction between "a", "l", and "1" at small sizes
- Weight: Medium or Semi-Bold for the wordmark; Regular for supporting text
- Letter-spacing: slightly open (not tight/compressed)

### Forbidden

- Serif typefaces
- Script or handwritten styles
- Decorative/novelty fonts
- Mixing more than one typeface in the wordmark

## 5. Color Palette

### Primary Direction

The palette should feel **technical, trustworthy, and modern** — not corporate-cold or startup-playful.

| Role    | Guidance                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary | A saturated but not neon blue, teal, or indigo — the "graph/data" color family. Should work on both light and dark backgrounds.                                                                                 |
| Accent  | A warm complement — amber, coral, or green — used sparingly for the "AI/intelligence" element (e.g., the distinct node in Proposal A, the transformation point in Proposal B, the lens interior in Proposal C). |
| Neutral | Near-black for text (#1a1a2e range), warm-gray for secondary (#6b7280 range), white/off-white for backgrounds.                                                                                                  |

### Constraints

- Primary and accent must have ≥ 4.5:1 contrast ratio against white (WCAG AA)
- The logo must remain legible in full monochrome (no color-dependent meaning)
- Maximum 3 colors in the mark (excluding black/white)
- Avoid pure red (error connotation in UI) and pure green (success connotation in UI)

## 6. What to Avoid

| Avoid                                      | Reason                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Brain or neural-net imagery                | Overused in AI branding; implies AGI hype rather than practical tooling |
| Literal robot or chatbot icons             | neo4all is not a chatbot product                                        |
| Globe or world map                         | "for all" does not mean geographic; avoid cliché universality imagery   |
| Lock or shield                             | Security/governance is a feature, not the identity                      |
| Gradients as the sole design element       | Must work in flat/mono; gradients only as enhancement                   |
| Neo4j's own logo or close derivatives      | neo4all is independent; must not be confused with Neo4j Inc. branding   |
| Overly complex illustrations               | Must reduce to favicon without losing identity                          |
| Trendy effects (glassmorphism, 3D renders) | Date quickly; prefer timeless geometry                                  |

## 9. Evaluation Criteria

Proposals will be evaluated on:

1. **Memorability** — Can you sketch it from memory after seeing it once?
2. **Scalability** — Does it work at 16px and 400px equally well?
3. **Relevance** — Does it communicate "graph + AI + documents" without explanation?
4. **Distinctiveness** — Is it clearly not Neo4j, LangChain, or any competitor?
5. **Versatility** — Does it hold up in mono, on dark, at small sizes, and in context?
6. **Timelessness** — Will it still feel current in 5 years?
